EDITORIAL POLICY

Data sources & accuracy

Our goal is a broad, bilingual football reference. This expanded catalog is a research starting point; it is not fully verified or complete. We separate stable biography and club-history fields from season-dependent data.

Stable fieldsBirth date, nationality, founding year, historic career
Fields requiring updatesCurrent club, squad, contract, market value, season stats
Source linksEvery record should be checked against its linked reference and preferably an official source.
Missing dataBlank means unknown or not checked; it does not mean zero.

Recommended source hierarchy

  1. Official club, league, federation and competition websites for official names, stadiums, founding history, honors and squad announcements.
  2. Open structured datasets such as Wikidata for stable entity identifiers and basic facts; check individual references and its reuse terms.
  3. Licensed football APIs for squads, appearances, fixtures, standings and season statistics; coverage depends on the plan.
  4. Transfermarkt can be used as a human research reference where appropriate, but do not copy its database wholesale or scrape it automatically without permission.

Important launch checklist

  • Review birth dates, positions, club founding years and stadium names against the linked pages.
  • Add a last-reviewed date and source URL for every fact that can change.
  • Do not add player images, crests or logos unless you have permission or a license that permits display.
  • Use a licensed provider for current squads and season statistics instead of manually guessing them.

Sources used in this expanded build

  • Egyptian Football Association — club directory Official club names and listed founding years; directory covers multiple divisions.
  • Egyptian Professional Clubs Association Competition and participating-club context.
  • Al Ahly — official first-team page Squad-list reference; current registration can change.
  • FC Barcelona — Hamza Abdelkarim profile Official profile for basic biography details.
  • Egyptian FA — U20 national-team listing Historical youth-national-team roster reference, not a current-club source.
  • UEFA Youth League — Arsenal squad, Real Madrid squad, Manchester United squad Official youth-squad references; these lists change by season.
  • The FA Full-Time — New Malden Town Confirms a team listing in the Leatherhead and District Sunday Football League. It does not verify Manuel Remon's registration or transfer.

A source link is a starting point for checking, not proof that every field on a record has been independently verified. We do not copy proprietary databases or guess missing values.
